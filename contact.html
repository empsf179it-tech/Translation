Frequently Asked Questions

What languages do you translate?

We cover over 100 languages, including major global languages like Spanish, Mandarin, French, German, Arabic, and Japanese, as well as many specialized regional dialects.

How long does a translation project typically take?

Turnaround time depends on the project size and complexity. Standard delivery is 2,000 words per day per linguist, but we also offer expedited services for urgent requests.

Do you offer certified translations?

Yes, we provide fully certified translations for legal, immigration, and official documents, accepted by government agencies and institutions worldwide.

How do you ensure translation quality?

We use a rigorous multi-step QA process involving initial translation, secondary review by a native-speaking editor, and final automated terminology checks.

What is Translation Memory (TM)?

TM is a secure database that stores your previously translated segments. This ensures brand consistency across projects and reduces costs for repeated text.

How do you handle confidential documents?

Security is paramount. We sign non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) and use enterprise-grade encryption to secure your data throughout the entire lifecycle.

Can you translate my website directly?

Yes, we offer seamless website localization. We can integrate with your CMS via API or work directly with your exported HTML/XML files.

What file formats do you support?

We support almost all modern file formats, including Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PPT), PDF, Adobe InDesign, HTML, XML, and JSON.

How is the cost of a translation calculated?

Pricing is typically calculated on a per-word basis. The exact rate depends on the language pair, technical complexity, and required turnaround time.

Do you use machine translation or human translators?

We offer pure human translation for marketing and nuanced content, as well as AI-assisted translation with human post-editing for high-volume technical data.

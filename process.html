The Localization Lifecycle

A transparent six-step methodology ensuring consistency and speed.

01

Project Analysis

We review file formats, extract text, and establish word counts against translation memory to maximize cost efficiency.

02

Linguist Assignment

The project is matched with a dedicated native translator possessing specific expertise in the subject matter.

03

Translation

The core translation process begins, utilizing approved client glossaries and style guides for absolute consistency.

04

Editing & Review

A second, independent linguist reviews the translation line-by-line against the source text to ensure complete accuracy.

05

Quality Assurance

Automated tools verify formatting, identify missing tags, and ensure numerical and terminological exactness.

06

Final Delivery

Files are reconstructed in their original format, finalized by the project manager, and delivered securely to you.

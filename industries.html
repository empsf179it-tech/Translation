Legal Translation Solutions

Translation Requirements

Absolute precision is required. We utilize sworn translators with active legal backgrounds who understand the specific jurisdictional terminology of the target country.

Document Types

  • Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)
  • Terms of Service & Privacy Policies
  • Litigation and Court Transcripts
  • Intellectual Property & Patents

Quality Considerations

Multi-step review by senior legal linguists. Certification and notarization available for official use in foreign courts.

Security

Encrypted file transfers, strict NDA enforcement across all staff, and isolated secure translation environments.

Medical & Life Sciences

Translation Requirements

Must comply with international regulatory bodies (FDA, EMA). We deploy subject-matter experts (SMEs) with advanced degrees in life sciences.

Document Types

  • Instructions for Use (IFU)
  • Clinical Trial Protocols
  • Patient Informed Consents
  • Regulatory Submissions

Quality Considerations

ISO 13485 compliant processes. Includes mandatory back-translation and reconciliation steps to verify 100% accuracy.

Localization Needs

Adapting medical device software interfaces to local markets without compromising safety or regulatory standards.

Technology & Software

Translation Requirements

Requires handling code structures, variables, and continuous integration workflows. Translators must be tech-savvy.

Document Types

  • UI Strings (.json, .xml, .po)
  • API Documentation
  • Knowledge Base / FAQs
  • Release Notes

Quality Considerations

Linguistic Quality Assurance (LQA) testing directly within the localized software environment to catch contextual errors.

Localization Needs

Agile localization pipelines allowing daily string updates synced automatically with your GitHub/GitLab repositories.
